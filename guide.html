How to use
ProposalKit

ProposalKit is G2 Group's internal pricing tool. It calculates client fees from your team's salary data, applies a multiplier, and packages everything into a professional proposal — with Excel and PDF export built in.

The system is organised around four agencies (G2Mena, GreyDoha, Pin&Notch, 8Mena), each with their own positions, clients, and proposals.

First-Time Setup

Follow this order when setting up a new agency or starting fresh. Each step depends on the one before it.

1
Set up your company
Go to Companies and confirm your agency is listed. Upload the agency logo here — it appears on all proposals and PDF exports.
2
Add your positions
Go to Positions, filter by your agency, and add roles with their monthly salaries. These are internal costs — not shared with clients.
3
Add your client
Go to Clients and create a record. Include a contact name and email for reference.
4
Create a proposal
Go to New Proposal. Select the agency, client, multiplier, and currency, then add position lines. Fees calculate live as you type.

Clients

Every proposal is linked to a client record. The client name is snapshotted at creation time, so renaming a client later won't affect historical proposals.

Tip: If a client isn't in the list, click the + button next to the client dropdown to add one in a new tab, then refresh.

Positions

Positions are the salary database. Each position belongs to one agency and has a monthly salary. All fee calculations derive from this salary.

Note: Salary data is internal — it never appears on client-facing exports. Proposals snapshot the salary at creation time, so updating a position later won't change existing proposals.

Rate Card

A read-only reference view showing all active positions with their hourly, daily, monthly, and annual rates at a chosen multiplier and currency. Use it to sense-check rates before building a proposal.

Creating a Proposal

1
Select agency and client
Both required. Changing the agency filters the available positions below.
2
Enter project name
e.g. Social Media Retainer Q4. Appears on all exports.
3
Choose currency and multiplier
Default currency is QAR. The multiplier is applied to every monthly salary to arrive at the charged rate.
4
Add position lines
Click Add Position. Select from the dropdown to pre-fill salary and title, or enter custom values. Set FTE (1.0 = full time). Fees calculate live.
5
Save
Click Save Proposal. Created with status Draft. Export or update status from the proposal page.

Versioning & Cloning

New Version

Creates a copy with an incremented version number (v2, v3…) for the same client and project. The original is preserved. Use when a client asks for changes.

Clone

Creates a completely independent copy with "(Copy)" in the name — starts as a fresh v1 draft. Use as a starting point for a similar scope with a different client.

Exporting

Print / PDF
